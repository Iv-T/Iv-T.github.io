I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Simon Fraser University](https://www.sfu.ca/), affiliated with the [GrUVi Lab](https://gruvi.cs.sfu.ca/) and the [3DLG Lab](https://3dlg-hcvc.github.io/).
I am honored to be supervised by Prof. [Manolis Savva](https://msavva.github.io/).
My research interests broadly span computer graphics, particularly 3D scene generation, egocentric world understanding, and human-centric interactions.

I received my M.Sc. in Computer Science from Simon Fraser University in 2025. Prior to that, I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science from SFU in 2023 with First Class Distinction.
I was also honored with the [Governor General’s Silver Medal](https://www.gg.ca/en/honours/recipients/116-113453), awarded for outstanding academic achievement.
